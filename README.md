# BigQuery Data Exploration Project
Used data from the [U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development](https://www.hud.gov/) in Google's BigQuery SQL environment to [analyze data and answer questions about the homeless population of the United States from 2012 to 2018](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1UkihoyJ9RjEaE2_HZ6CFthHe9pl8gETmrSH2wH9oMWw/edit?usp=sharing).

The project included cleaning the data and following a worksheet to answer questions involving the Continuum of Care areas listed in the dataset by writing SQL queries.

## Findings
 - The three highest Continuum of Care (CoC) areas in the year 2018 were:
	 + San Jose/Santa Clara City & County CoC
	 + Oregon Balance of State CoC
	 + Las Vegas/Clark County CoC
 - The three top locations in terms of number of sheltered homeless people in 2018 were:
	 + Philadelphia CoC
	 + Reno, Sparks/Washoe County CoC
	 + Indianapolis CoC
 - Between the years of 2012 and 2018 the number of unsheltered homeless people in Delaware had not significantly increased (though, it should be noted, it also did not significantly *decrease*)
 - The top seven states in terms of overall homelessness were:
	 + California
	 + Florida
	 + New York
	 + Michigan
	 + Illinois
	 + Maryland
	 + Pennsylvania
 - Overall homelessness was not correlated with state population
 - Only two locations had less than 2 % of all homeless be counted as unsheltered:
	 + Springfield/Hampden County CoC (1.30 %)
	 + Nassau, Suffolk Counties CoC (1.34 %)
